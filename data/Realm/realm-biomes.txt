##
## The one thing neither source can answer. The map says what colour a stretch
## of ground is; the client says which creatures live in which biome; nothing
## joins the two. This file is the join, and it is a player's reading of the
## map rather than a fact read out of a file — so it says who said so and when,
## the same way data/Fame/availability-overrides.txt does.
