## What does not belong on a map of a place.
##
## A walked recording keeps whatever happened to be standing there. Some of it
## is the realm - trees, walls, monuments - and some of it is the afternoon:
## other players and their pets, the bags their kills dropped, an attack that
## was still in the air. The second kind comes back every time more of the
## realm is walked, so it is turned away here rather than deleted afterwards,
## and the map is built without it from the start.
##
## Four kinds of line, and anything after ## is a note:
##
##   class NAME   turn away everything the client files under that class
##   id NAME      turn away one thing by the name the client gives it; * matches
##   keep NAME    keep this one whatever else says, and it wins over everything
##   life NAME    take this creature out of a place's list of what lives there
##
## Written 2026-09-02.

## Anything drawn from an empty square.
##
## Most of the machinery of the realm is placed as an object, because that is
## the only way the client knows to put something on a tile: the spawner that
## puts the monsters there, the anchor a guardian paces round, the beam it
## throws, the marker where the loot fell. All of them are drawn from nothing,
## so nine thousand of them were being sorted by depth and written into the
## standing layer every build in order to draw exactly nothing.
##
## The beacons are drawn from nothing as well and are not lost with them: they
## are read out of the store by name and carried in atlas.json, and the page
## draws them from there.
