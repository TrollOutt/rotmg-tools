## When in a realm's run the seasonal events bring their creatures out.
##
## Which creatures appear, and how a realm's score decides when, is not in the
## client's files: the server watches the realm's score (the percentage meter
## that fills as creatures die) and brings the creature out when it passes a
## mark. So this is what players have written down, kept here where it can be
## read and corrected, like data/Items/status-effects.txt. The client does say
## which aliens belong to which invasion wave; tools/build-realm-events.js reads
## that itself and joins it to this file.
##
## tools/build-realm-events.js writes web/assets/index/realm-events.json.
##
## Two kinds of line:
##
## event|id|name|when it runs|begins at (% of the realm's score)|summary|picture|sources
## at|event id|who|scores|where|what happens|sources
##
##   who      an Index id ("enemy:The Gardener"), or a plain name. A plain name is
##            joined to the Index creature of that name when there is one; a
##            creature the Index has not got (a reskin, a portal, a group of
##            mercenaries) stays as the name it was given.
##   scores   the percentages, "15,50,85". Left empty when the creature comes
##            out for another reason (the kills before it), which the next
##            column says. "?" when the source says it is score-based and gives
##            no figure.
##   sources  RealmEye pages (the part after /wiki/), comma separated, or
##            notes:<file> for this project's own update notes (data/Updates).
##
## Written from RealmEye's pages and release notes, read 2026-10-12. The figures
## are the community's, not the client's: where two pages differ the later
## release note wins, and the line says so.
