## What a status effect is, and what it does.
##
## The client says which effects a thing grants or inflicts, on whom and for
## how long. It never says what they are worth - Curse is implemented in the
## game's code, not declared in its files - nor which picture the game draws
## for them. This file is that knowledge, written down where it can be read and
## corrected, like data/Fame/availability-overrides.txt, instead of buried in a
## source file as magic numbers. tools/build-status-effects.js joins it with
## the client (who inflicts what, who is immune) and cuts the icons.
##
## The mechanics are taken from the RotMG wiki's Status Effects page and put in
## our own words:
##   https://www.realmeye.com/wiki/status-effects
##
## effect|tone|icon|model|on a player|on an enemy
##
##   effect   the name the client uses
##   tone     good or bad, for whoever carries it
##   icon     the client's own picture: atlas#index in client-data/spritesheet.bin.
##            The game draws these from code, so which one goes with which effect
##            was found by matching RealmEye's icons pixel for pixel against the
##            client's lofiInterface sheets (2026-10-04).
##   model    what the damage model applies, as "quantity op value" pairs:
##              rate     shots a second           (x factor)
##              damage   a weapon shot's damage   (x factor, before armour)
##              def      the target's defence     (x factor, + or - flat, "zero")
##              taken    damage after armour      (x factor)
##              bleed    life lost a second       (default when the source says no figure)
##              range    how far a shot travels   (x factor)
##              speed    walking speed            (x factor)
##              shots    an enemy's shots a volley (x factor)
##              dealt    an enemy's damage        (x factor)
##              hpregen / mpregen  + a second
##   last two what it does, said once for each side; blank where it does nothing
##            or the wiki says nothing.

## --- stat changes ---
##
## Not one effect but eight, one a stat, and the size comes from the item that
## grants it, not from here. Two rules belong to the effect: buffs from
## different sources do not add, the strongest wins; repeats from one source
## are each worth half the one before. The icons are the client's own, one a
## stat, drawn red when the change is a loss.
Stat Increase|good|lofiInterfaceBig#296||A stat raised for a while; the strongest of several sources wins, and repeats from one source are each worth half the last.|
Stat Reduction|bad|lofiInterfaceBig#296||A stat lowered for a while, by the same rules.|

## --- immunities the client does not declare ---
##
## immune|enemy|effect,effect. Bosses declare stun, stasis, paralyze, petrify,
## daze and slow immunity themselves; armour break and curse immunity are in
## the game's code.
