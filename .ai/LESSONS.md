# Lessons (append-only)

## Incident format

`YYYY-MM-DD — context — observed failure — durable prevention — verification`

## 2026-09-22 — orchestration hardening

Worktrees do not carry canonical helpers, and Windows argv is not a safe review-diff transport. Doctor/preflight checks, stdin/file review transport, and regression tests are required safeguards.

## 2026-09-22 — provider recovery

Usage, session, rate, and transport failures must be recorded as temporary availability facts rather than retried blindly or treated as a permanent provider ban. A successful later invocation restores health.
